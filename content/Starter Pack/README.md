# Alien Studio Starter Pack

Drum one-shots and MIDI clips that come with Alien Studio, so there is something to play
before you load your own sounds. You may use them in your music, free, with no royalties.

None of this material was made by the Alien Studio project. It comes from the openly
licensed sources below, and `manifest.csv` lists every file with its source, license and
the original file it was made from. `tools/build_starter_pack.py` in the Alien Studio
repository rebuilds this folder from pinned downloads.

## What is here

| Folder | Contents | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Drums/Kicks`, `Snares`, `Hats`, `Toms`, `Cymbals` | Acoustic kit one-shots: the kit's close, overhead and room microphones mixed into one stereo hit | Virtuosity Drums | CC0 1.0 |
| `Drums/Percussion` | Hand percussion: shaker, tambourine, congas, bongos, cowbell and more (close microphone) | Virtuosity Drums | CC0 1.0 |
| `Drums/Claps` | Group and solo hand claps | Versilian Community Sample Library | CC0 1.0 |
| `MIDI/Chord Progressions` | Ten progressions in C major and ten in A minor | free-midi-chords | MIT |
| `MIDI/Drum Grooves` | The first four bars of eighteen grooves in different styles, played by drummers on an electronic kit (General MIDI drum notes) | Groove MIDI Dataset | CC BY 4.0 |

Samples were trimmed, faded, normalized to -1 dBFS and saved as 16-bit WAV. Grooves were
cut to their first four bars. Chord progressions are unchanged.
